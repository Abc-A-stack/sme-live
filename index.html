Connected Architecture

Why Businesses Choose YES SME

Replace fragmented spreadsheets, disconnected POS terminals, and manual bookkeeping with a single, synchronized source of business truth.

Real-Time Synchronization

When an item sells at your physical counter, inventory deducts immediately, an accounting voucher auto-posts, commission attributes to the salesperson, and online stock updates simultaneously.

Zero-Downtime Offline POS

Power cuts or internet disruptions will never halt your retail sales. Cashiers can continue barcode scanning, printing receipts, and billing offline, with automatic sync upon reconnection.

Bangladesh Native Integrations

Built directly for the Bangladeshi business ecosystem with bKash, Nagad, Pathao, Steadfast, REDX, NBR VAT/Mushak reports, and native BDT currency formatting.

Conversational AI Insights

Ask business intelligence questions in natural Bangla or English ("আজকের মোট বিক্রি কত?", "কার কাছে সবচেয়ে বেশি টাকা বাকি?") to get instant live answers without opening spreadsheets.

Target & Commission Engine

Motivate staff with automated employee sales attribution, individual targets, leaderboards, and tiered commission calculations integrated straight into the monthly payroll.

Dedicated Mobile Apps

Empower your brand with dedicated Android and iOS mobile applications included in Medium and High packages, bringing an omnichannel shopping experience to your customers.
